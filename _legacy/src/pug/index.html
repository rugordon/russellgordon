Whispers From The Dark

Amy Peters is a young girl with a gift and a tragic start in life. Sent away to boarding school she has to fend for herself without anyone to support her. When she arrives at Carisbrooke School in Dorset, one in a growing list of schools, something awakens in the surrounding woods. At the same time something awakens in Amy as well. A power that she doesn't know she possesses.
